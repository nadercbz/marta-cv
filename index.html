Hi OPEN team,

my name is Marta Joanna, I'm 21 and I live in Berlin. I would love to join your Karma Yogi team.

Why OPEN
What I like most about OPEN is that it doesn't feel like a regular Pilates studio. You describe it as a place to move, feel, heal and let go, and you can feel that the moment you walk into one of your studios. Everyone is welcome, no matter who they are. I would love to help keep exactly that feeling alive.

Why Karma Yogi
A class only really lands when the room is ready for it. Mats lined up, blankets folded, props back in place, a calm moment before the next group comes in, especially before a Sound session. That's the part behind the scenes I want to take care of. At Budni and in hotel and event service I learned to keep a place clean, calm and welcoming even on busy days, and to stay calm in stressful situations. I speak German, English and Polish, so I can welcome everyone who comes to OPEN in their own language.

Why me
Sport has always been a big part of my life. I love moving and I'm into sports nutrition. Lately I tried several classes at OPEN and I really loved them. Especially the atmosphere: the gong, the singing bowls and the music somehow lead you back to something very natural inside yourself. After work they help me calm down and come back to myself. That's exactly why I want to grow in my own practice with you, in yoga and Pilates.

A few quick questions:
1. Could I help out at 2 to 3 of your studios?
2. How many shifts per week do you usually expect?
3. Is it okay to take a week off now and then when I travel?
4. Can Karma Yogis join Pilates classes too, or only yoga?
5. If you like the idea, I could film short TikToks while I prepare the studio, like "POV: how I prepare the studio for the next day", as a small promotion for OPEN. Only if it fits into my shift.

My CV is attached. I'd be happy to come by for a short chat or a trial shift.
